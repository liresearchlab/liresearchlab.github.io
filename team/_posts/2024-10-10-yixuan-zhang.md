I finished my Bachelor’s study at Donghua University followed by a Master’s study at TU Delft. My research interests focus on the computational design and understanding of materials for all-solid-state batteries, particularly the electrode–electrolyte interfaces and point defects, where I combine first-principles calculations, atomistic simulations, high-throughput computation, and machine learning to probe the unknowns and uncertainties.
